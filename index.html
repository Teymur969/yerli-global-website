    OUR STORY

    Yerli means local. Homegrown. Made in-house. Everything we put out is made under our own roof, from the first beat to the final cut.

    We started in 2022 as Yerli Records, founded in Baku by Teymur, Kamal and Khidir. Then the artists and clients started coming from further away. The label grew into a production house, and the name grew with it. Yerli Global.

    TWO PILLARS
